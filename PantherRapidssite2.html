About Panther Rapids

Investing in Charleston’s future Panther Rapids is an exciting multi-phase recreational project designed to transform Charleston's Embarras River into a vibrant hub for outdoor activities, economic growth, and environmental restoration. This initiative will provide a dynamic space for kayaking, riverside relaxation, trails, and community gathering spots, benefiting residents and attracting visitors from across the region. The project represents a strategic public-private partnership opportunity with significant ROI potential and community benefits spanning recreation, education, tourism, and environmental conservation.

Our Vision

Recreational Excellence

Create a premier kayak surfing rapid that will attract enthusiasts from across the Midwest, complemented by a riverside beach area, multi-use trails, and river access points for diverse water activities.

Commercial Vibrancy

Develop riverside cafes and outdoor eating areas with scenic views, equipment rental facilities, and gathering spaces that create revenue streams while enhancing visitor experiences.

Environmental Stewardship

Preserve and enhance riparian buffers, implement innovative stormwater management solutions, and create habitat improvements that demonstrate sustainable development principles.

Summary Economic Benefits

Panther Rapids is expected to directly inject tourism dollars and jobs into the local economy, stimulate higher property values and new business development (indirect effects), and create multiplier-driven growth in income and employment (induced effects). In similar projects, these benefits have proven substantial. For instance, Reno’s $1.5M whitewater park was projected to pay for itself within 3 years via new visitor spending (extending tourist stays and attracting new visitors)sfgate.com. Likewise, Golden’s kayak park yields multi-million-dollar returns annually for local businessesboaterparks.com. Charleston’s investment, while moderate, should yield a strong return in economic activity, improved tax base, and enhanced community well-being.

The project will catalyze new business development, with 8-12 new enterprises expected within a two-mile radius by Year 5, creating an additional 35-50 jobs and generating $175,000-$225,000 in annual tax revenue.
